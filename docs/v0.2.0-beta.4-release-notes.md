# Webcam QR Scanner v0.2.0-beta.4

Limited Windows x64 / Phone-to-PC pre-release. Latest stable remains v0.1.1.
Previous beta tags and assets are retained; this release does not merge into main.

## What's changed

- Send the same QR link again intentionally: after **Received by PC**, choose
  **Scan another QR**, scan that code again and select **Send to PC**. The new
  scan creates a fresh encrypted message ID. Double taps and uncertain deliveries
  still reuse their original attempt; rescanning cannot bypass replay protection.
- Use the Windows system arrow in desktop menus, pairing/recovery dialogs,
  camera view and monitor picker. Screen-region selection keeps its crosshair;
  title-bar and resize cursors remain native.

## Update

Fully exit the old QR Scanner from the system tray. Extract the entire ZIP and
open QR-Scanner.exe; refresh [qrwebcam.vercel.app](https://qrwebcam.vercel.app/)
on your phone. Pairings in the same Windows account are retained. Normal use
does not require PowerShell, a Vercel account or an automation bypass secret.
SHA256SUMS.txt covers the ZIP and the executable inside it.

privacy/security review and independent qualified protocol review remain open
before main/stable promotion. The EXE is unsigned and may trigger SmartScreen.
Protected video may appear black in screen capture; insufficient original QR
pixels cannot be recovered by enlargement. Short-lived delivery receipts can
be missed when a phone tab is suspended or reloaded; success is not then claimed.
