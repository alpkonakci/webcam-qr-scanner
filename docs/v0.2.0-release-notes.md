# Webcam QR Scanner v0.2.0

Windows x64 stable release, published under MIT with no activation or license check.

## Included

- Camera and screen-area QR scanning; QR creation, PNG export and copy.
- Phone camera and local image/paste QR decoding; encrypted Send to PC.
- Turkish/English controls, keyboard navigation, settings and connection status.
- Canonical URL identity shared by destination confirmation and browser opening.
- Pair revocation serialized against message enqueue; revoked queues excluded
  from new claims. Already-in-flight claims/visible confirmations cannot be retracted.
- Replay protection, authenticated receipts and nonce-based production CSP.

## Updating

Exit the old app through its tray menu. Extract the complete
`Webcam-QR-Scanner-v0.2.0-windows-x64.zip`, then open `QR-Scanner.exe`.
Refresh https://qrwebcam.vercel.app on the phone. No Vercel account, PowerShell
setup or bypass secret is required. Pairing persists in the same Windows
account and browser/origin; different browsers and installed web apps may
have separate storage. Re-pair if switching from a preview address or Sandbox.

The EXE is unsigned; Windows may show a publisher/reputation warning. Compare
the download with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Source and dependency/license material are
included. Previous releases remain available for rollback.

## Evidence and security limitations

The functional candidate passed 348 desktop and 148 web tests, including
real PostgreSQL two-session revocation contention. User-confirmed flows cover
Samsung Internet/S24 FE, Safari/iPhone 15 Plus and a clean Windows Sandbox.
Release-source CI and package/hash checks are recorded separately.

**No independent human protocol/cryptography audit has been performed.**
Independent AI code reviews are not human review or security certification.
The maintainer explicitly accepted this limitation for the stable release.
Stable means the maintainer's supported release channel, not a guarantee of
being vulnerability-free. Full browser/OS-version and installed-PWA matrices,
dynamic VoiceOver/TalkBack coverage, rate-limit coverage and live valid-token
cross-device abuse testing are not comprehensively certified.

ACK loss may leave delivery uncertain; this is not a durable process-crash
recovery queue. Keep the phone status page open and check status before retrying.
Unsupported hard-link storage fails closed during replay-ledger initialization.
