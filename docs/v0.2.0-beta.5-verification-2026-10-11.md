support, updated dependencies and beta.5 packaging. No database migration,
credential rotation or change to pairing permissions is included.

## Visual and user checks

The final extracted beta.5 EXE was opened on Windows: correct icon, disabled
maximize button, all four home cards visible and existing pairing retained.
Create QR produced the preview and Copy QR displayed success. Save PNG through
the native dialog produced an RGB 481 x 481 image; OpenCV decoded it back to
exactly `https://example.com/beta5-qr`.

The earlier candidate visual pass also covered Unicode/multiline text, invalid
and empty inputs, cancelling Save and returning to the home screen. See
`qr-creation-mobile-screenshots-2026-10-10.md`.

The user confirmed on a real phone that a desktop-created QR scans, the supplied
light-on-dark poster opens through Choose image, and a pasted second QR opens.
This is targeted acceptance, not a fresh full iPhone/Android pairing matrix on
the exact final ZIP. Existing core-flow acceptance remains historical evidence.
