## Open gates / limits

Physical iPhone/Android regression of this exact candidate, forced deployed
Realtime fallback/healthy-resync timing, broader Auth/RPC/error-path/log/CSP
privacy review and independent qualified protocol/cryptography review remain
open. Prior beta user-attested device tests are not reclassified as beta.3 tests.

Persistent replay records do not defend against malicious same-user rollback
or deletion. Acceptance is at-most-once; a crash between persistence, ACK and
UI callback can lose delivery. Missed short-lived ACKs cannot establish success
after expiry. Background phone suspension/reload/other tabs retain that limit.
Server cleanup is probabilistic; local expired digests prune on later messages.
There is no immediate physical-erasure guarantee.
